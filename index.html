Most soft drinks start with sugar and work backwards. We start with water — lightly sparkling spring water — and add pressed fruit for taste, a B-vitamin complex for energy metabolism, and minerals drawn from deep ocean water for everyday hydration.

That's the whole ingredients story. Nothing to hide, so the can says it out loud: natural hydration drink, no added sugars, good on the go.

04 — HOW TO GO GOOD

Three moves.

STEP / 01
Chill it

Fridge-cold is good. Buried in ice is better. GO GOOD is built to be drunk cold, when the bubbles are at their sharpest.

STEP / 02
Crack & pour

Straight from the can works anywhere. Got a glass and ice? Pour slow and watch the colour rise — no syrup, just fruit.

STEP / 03
Go

Desk, trail, gym, train. One can, no crash — hydration with vitamins and minerals that travels as fast as you do.
